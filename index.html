Coral Guards

Solar-Powered Smart Buoy for Real-Time Marine Water Quality Monitoring and Coral Reef Protection

A renewable-energy marine monitoring platform that continuously measures environmental conditions to help protect coral reef ecosystems.

Discover More

Introduction

Coral reefs are among the most valuable marine ecosystems on Earth. They support biodiversity, protect coastlines from erosion, and provide habitats for thousands of marine organisms.

Unfortunately, coral reefs are increasingly threatened by pollution, climate change, and declining water quality.

Coral Guards is a smart solar-powered buoy capable of continuously monitoring marine water quality and transmitting real-time environmental data for scientific analysis and conservation efforts.

Problem Statement

Traditional marine monitoring depends on manual sampling and field surveys, making it expensive, slow, and unable to provide continuous environmental information.

Main Challenges

  • Limited monitoring frequency.
  • High operational costs.
  • Difficult access to remote marine environments.
  • Lack of continuous real-time water quality data.
  • Delayed detection of coral reef threats.

Hardware Components

ESP32 Development Board

The main controller responsible for collecting sensor data, processing it, and transmitting it wirelessly.

NEO-6M GPS Module

Provides accurate positioning of the buoy for tracking and monitoring its deployment location.

DS18B20 Temperature Sensor

Measures water temperature with high precision to detect environmental changes.

TDS Sensor

Measures the concentration of dissolved solids to evaluate water quality.

Turbidity Sensor

Detects suspended particles and water clarity, indicating pollution levels.

Solar Panel

Provides renewable energy to power the smart buoy, ensuring long-term operation with minimal maintenance.

ESP32 Camera

used for live underwater image streaming to visually monitor coral reef health.

Battery

Stores energy for the smart buoy, ensuring continuous operation.

Methodology

Coral Guards continuously collects environmental data using multiple sensors installed on a floating smart buoy.

The ESP32 processes all sensor readings and sends them wirelessly to a desktop monitoring application where users can visualize real-time information.

The entire system operates using renewable solar energy, allowing long-term deployment with minimal maintenance.
